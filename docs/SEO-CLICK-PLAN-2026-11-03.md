## 2. The diagnosis

The 29 October rewrite made titles **action-first** and that was read as **verb-first**. It produced
things like:

- `Access TallyPrime From Anywhere` for a query of `tallycloudaccess`
- `Record Vouchers in Tally Prime` for a query of `types of voucher in tally`
- `Lock Data in TallyPrime: How To` for a query of `how to lock tally data for previous year`
- `Work Anywhere` / `The Basics` as the *end* of a title

Verb-first moves the one thing the searcher has already decided (their words) out of the visible
head of the snippet. On a position-2 result that costs half the clicks. On a position-9 result it
costs all of them.

### The rule for everything below

> **The head of the title must be the query. The action goes in the tail.**
>
> `Tally Cloud Access: How It Works & Costs` — query first, promise second.
> `How to Lock Data in TallyPrime (Previous Year)` — query shape first, qualifier second.

Three supporting rules:

1. **`How to` belongs at the front** when the query has it. It is part of the phrase, not an action word.
2. **The qualifier people actually type goes in the title**, not only the description
   ("Previous Year", "Navi Mumbai", "7.2", "GSTIN"), because the title is the bolded text.
3. **The brand suffix is the disposable tail.** `seoMetadata()` appends `| Sarvadnya Infotech LLP`
   (25 chars). A title at 62 chars loses the suffix in the SERP, not the query — that is an
   acceptable trade and is already how `scripts/seo-audit.mjs` treats it (WARN, non-fatal).

The 27 changed URLs carried **8,541 impressions and 63 clicks — 0.74% CTR** in this window. The site
average of 1.51% is carried by the homepage (6.1%) and brand queries; the content pages are far below

| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| Target | 2.50% | 214 | 305 | **+151 (+98%)** |
| Ambitious | 3.00% | 256 | 347 | **+193 (+125%)** |

Assumptions, stated so they can be broken: impressions and positions hold, Google re-crawls and
re-writes the snippets, and nothing else on the site regresses. Positions 3–6 do not need smarter
copy to reach 2.5%; they need the query in the head of the title. That is the bet.

**Do not judge this before 21 November.** Google takes 2–6 weeks to display a rewritten title, and
Search Console's own reporting lags a further 2–3 days. Anything measured sooner is noise.
