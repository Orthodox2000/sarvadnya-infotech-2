## 4 — Urgent: mobile hidden-menu scroll jump

Owner: "for mobile navbar hiden menu, scrollbar is not smooth for mobiel, it first scoll to bottom of
website , then scrolls menu".

- Reproduced at 360 × 800: opening the drawer at `scrollY 300` jumped to `2245` (+1945 px, the drawer's
  height); the sticky header/wrapper grew from 66 px to 2011 px and the document from 5308 to 7253 px.
  Cause: the drawer was an in-flow child of the sticky header, so scroll anchoring pushed the page.
- `app/components/SiteChrome.tsx`: drawer is now an absolute overlay (`top-full`, capped
  `max-h-[calc(100dvh-5rem)]`, own `overflow-y-auto overscroll-contain`, `role="dialog"`) with a fixed
  tap-outside backdrop. Added a `mobileOpen` effect that locks `body.style.overflow` and restores the
  previous value on cleanup; Escape now closes it too.
- Verified in headless Chrome: `scrollY 300 -> 300`, document height and header stay unchanged, drawer
  scrolls internally (719/1944 px), scrolling it leaves the page still, backdrop present, Escape
  restores body scroll.
