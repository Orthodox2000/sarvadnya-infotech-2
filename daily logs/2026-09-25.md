# Daily Log — 2026-09-25

## Session: Google Search Console thumbnail URL compatibility fix

**Context:** Google Search Console reported “Thumbnail could not be reached” for the homepage video. The 2026-09-22 opaque-JPEG poster conversion addressed the separate “Thumbnail is transparent” report. This session addressed the legacy thumbnail URL and the media header matchers so Google can fetch the current asset and receive the intended headers.

---

## WHAT

- `next.config.js` now permanently redirects the deleted legacy URL `/video-thumbnail.png` to `/video-thumbnail.jpg`. Next.js emits this as an HTTP `308` redirect.
- Removed the literal `$` from the custom image and video `headers()` source matchers. The compiled matchers now cover the thumbnail and MP4 assets instead of requiring an unintended trailing character.
- Preserved the existing opaque JPEG poster, raw-video `robots.txt`/`X-Robots-Tag` policy, and the intentional no-`VideoObject` strategy. No visual or indexing-policy change was introduced by this session.

## WHY

- The GSC message is an asset accessibility/crawlability problem, not a robots.txt error. A permanent compatibility redirect prevents stale links or historical GSC records from ending at a 404 after the PNG replacement.
- The malformed header source expressions could prevent the intended image/video response headers from being applied. Correcting them keeps the current thumbnail and MP4 delivery behavior explicit and testable.
- The redirect and matcher correction are deliberately limited to compatibility and delivery; the existing decision to keep the decorative hero video out of video-search indexing remains intact.
